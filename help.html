Frequently asked questions

Why is a match score or event missing?

The public screens display information that has been published to the app. Live events and final results may not appear until the responsible match officials or administrators have submitted and published them. Use the report form below for general issues, or open that match's details and choose Report a problem to attach your report to a particular match.

How do I view a club or player?

Open Teams to find club information, squad details and club performance. Open Players to search registered player records and view available player profiles and statistics.

How do match reminders work?

First enable Get alerts and allow notifications in your browser. Then use Remind me beside an upcoming match. The reminder is scheduled for one hour before kick-off, provided the match remains scheduled and there is enough time to create it.

Can I use the app without internet?

The app can open using its saved app shell and previously cached public information. New scores, news, media and other updates require an internet connection. An offline copy may not contain the latest changes.

Does this app replace FIFA Connect?

No. Its intended role is to make published league information easier for the public to access. It is not a replacement for FIFA Connect or for the Association's official competition administration systems.

Report incorrect information or a problem

Send a short description to the moderation team. You do not need to sign in. Reports are reviewed by authorised staff; sending a report does not automatically change a score, squad or other published record.

Privacy and platform status

Reports are stored for review by authorised moderators. The report form uses a random code stored in this browser to help limit repeated submissions. It does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.

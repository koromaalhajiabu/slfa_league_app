Privacy notice

The SLFA League App is run by the Sierra Leone Football Association (SLFA) [confirm the operating body's full legal name]. This notice explains what personal information the app stores and why.

What we store

  • Predictions: the name you type, the score you predict, and a random code kept in your browser. The code stops one browser sending many entries. We do not ask for your email address or phone number to make a prediction.
  • Comments: the name you type and the comment text. Comments are shown only after an SLFA moderator approves them.
  • Problem reports: if you report a wrong score or player, the type of problem, the message you write, and the same random browser code. Reports are read by SLFA moderators.
  • Account agreement: if you create a staff account, the time you agreed to this notice and the version you agreed to.
  • Usage counts: the app records only the name of an action (for example, that the Matches tab was opened) and the time. It does not record your name, device, browser code or address. These counts help SLFA see which features are used.
  • Alerts: if you switch on alerts, a notification address for your browser and the clubs you choose to follow, so that you receive only alerts about those clubs.
  • Favourite clubs: if you choose favourite clubs, they are saved so they stay in place when you return.
  • Your browser: some choices, such as your name and language, are saved on your device only. You can delete them by clearing the app's site data in your browser.

Why we store it

To run the league app: to show the prediction leaderboard, to moderate comments and problem reports, to send alerts you asked for (including one-hour kick-off reminders for matches involving clubs), to remember your language and favourite clubs, to keep a record that you agreed to these terms, to see which features are used, and to keep copies of league records in case of loss.

Who can see it

  • Everyone can see approved comments and the names on the prediction leaderboard.
  • Everyone can see published results, the league table, current suspensions (with the player's name and club) and published disciplinary decisions.
  • Only SLFA Super Admins and Media Officers can see comments waiting for approval.
  • Only moderators can see problem reports. Only Super Admins and Developers can see usage counts.
  • Only Super Admins can download league data. Full downloads include staff account details and player records, so they are handled with care and every download is recorded.
  • We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Predictions and comments are kept for [SLFA to set the retention period, for example two seasons]. Problem reports are kept for [SLFA to set the period]. Usage counts are kept for [SLFA to set the period, for example 13 months]. Alert addresses are removed when they stop working or when you turn alerts off. Records of agreement are kept while the account exists.

League data copies (backups) are kept for [SLFA to set the period] and then deleted.

Your choices

  • Turn alerts off in the app, or block notifications for the site in your browser.
  • Clear the app's site data to remove the saved code, name and language from your device.
  • Ask us to remove a prediction, comment or problem report that includes your name: [SLFA contact email or form].

Children

Comments are public. Please do not post personal information about yourself or anyone else, and do not share anything about children. [SLFA to confirm how comments from under-18s are handled].

Staff accounts and medical information

Staff accounts store a name, an email address, a role and a sign-in. Player medical information is not shown in the public app. [SLFA to confirm who may access medical records, and how long they are kept].

Terms of use

  • Keep comments and predictions respectful. Do not post abuse, threats, hate, racism, spam, or anyone's personal details.
  • SLFA moderators may reject or remove any comment, and may stop a browser from sending more comments.
  • Official scores come only from confirmed results. Predictions and leaderboards are for fun and are not official competitions.
  • League information is provided as it is published. We try to keep it accurate. If you spot an error, tell us.
  • Club names, crests and other league material belong to their owners and to the SLFA. [SLFA to confirm the usage terms for crests].
  • We may update these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version.
